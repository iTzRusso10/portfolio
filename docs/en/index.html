FAQ

Straight answers.

How much does a website cost?

€250 all-inclusive for the first year: a custom business website with a .it domain and hosting, Call, WhatsApp and Maps buttons, and 2 rounds of changes. From the second year it's €50 a year, covering domain renewal, hosting and support.

Can I write to you in English?

Yes. Message me on WhatsApp or by email in English and we'll run the whole project remotely.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. I work remotely and don't have an office open to the public, so everything happens on WhatsApp, by phone or by email. To start, I only need your business name and a link to your Google profile or Instagram.

How fast is it?

The homepage draft arrives within one working day of your request. If you like it, the full site is live within 48 hours of receiving your texts and photos.

How do I pay?

No deposit. You pay only after you've seen and approved the finished site, in the way you prefer, and you get an invoice.

Who registers the domain?

I register the domain under my name and take care of renewals, so you don't have to manage anything.
